Do you know how fancy gelatin-based desserts used to be? From say, the mid-eighteenth century, jelly desserts were like those science desserts that are just a glowing green orb that gives off fragrant minty gas are today. FANCY. I made a blackberry-balsamic pâte and a blueberry-Meyer lemon-thyme pâte. I chose those things because they are all ingredients that I already had. My happiness in this life is in direct relation to how often I'm able to cook exciting things using only things I already have on hand. That shit makes me feel like a frontierswoman. Or like, a Gaulish clan leader (you could be a lady clan leader in Gaul). OR LIKE, A PRIESTESS IN A TEMPLE OF ISIS IN ANCIENT EGYPT BUT I'M REAL ENTERPRISING AND I HAVE A SIDE HUSTLE OF SELLING GUMMY CANDIES TO CUSH. Cooking with things I have on hand gives me a comforting though false hope that when America finally crumbles all the way down, I'll be able to last at least a couple days without having to go out and brave the looting. My Safeway is already kinda Beyond Thunderdome so it's pretty easy to picture.
