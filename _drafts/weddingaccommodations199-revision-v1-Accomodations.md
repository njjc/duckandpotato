We’ve reserved rooms at the lovely [Inn at Gig Harbor](http://www.innatgigharbor.com/ "Inn at Gig Harbor"). To reserve, please call the Inn at (253) 858-1111 and ask for the Monaghan-Campbell wedding. Rooms are $129 for 2 people and $10 per extra person. You can stay as many days as you like.

If you opt to stay elsewhere, whatever floats your boat, but the shuttle to the house on the day of the wedding will pick folks up at the Inn at Gig Harbor at 4pm. If you miss the shuttle, don’t miss the shuttle.

The shuttle will take people from the house back to the Inn at Gig Harbor at 11pm and at 1am.

### Transportation from SeaTac (Seattle Tacoma International Airport - airport code **SEA**)

Good news! There is a shuttle service that will pick you up from SeaTac and deposit you DIRECTLY at the Inn at Gig Harbor. The shuttle costs $16.50 each way to and from the Inn at Gig Harbor, and takes about 40 minutes to get to and from the airport, so keep that in mind. You don’t need a reservation to take the shuttle from the airport to the Inn at Gig Harbor, the directions to take the shuttle are here:

Departures from SeaTac are from the South Ground Transportation Lot outside of Door '00'. This is located at the south end of the Baggage Claim Area past Carousel #1. The shuttle vans, labeled “Kitsap Airporter” are normally in the parking lot outside of the waiting area approximately 15 minutes prior to departure times.

Our [schedules](http://www.kitsapairporter.com/AirporterSchedules/KitsapSeaTac/) are available at the ground transportation offices, from the BKA Desk in the South Arrival Hall, or from our Airporter drivers.

You’ll need to book shuttle reservations to return to the airport in advance, which you can do on the [Kitsap Airporter website](https://www.kitsapairporter.com/index.php/Reservations/KitsapSeaTac). Easy!
