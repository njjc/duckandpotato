### Transportation from SeaTac (Seattle Tacoma International Airport - airport code **SEA**)

Good news! There is a shuttle service that will pick you up from SeaTac airport and deposit you DIRECTLY at the Inn at Gig Harbor. The shuttle costs $16.50 each way (per person) to and from the Inn at Gig Harbor, and takes about 40 minutes each way, so keep that in mind. You don’t need a reservation to take the shuttle from the airport to the Inn at Gig Harbor, the directions to take the shuttle are below:

**Directions to take the shuttle from SeaTac Airport to the Inn at Gig Harbor:**

Departures from SeaTac are from the South Ground Transportation Lot outside of Door '00'. This is located at the south end of the Baggage Claim Area past Carousel #1. The shuttle vans, labeled “**Kitsap Airporter**” are normally in the parking lot outside of the waiting area approximately 15 minutes prior to departure times. Please check in with the agent at the Airporter Customer Service Counter near door #00 before boarding the bus.

Shuttle schedules from the airport are available [online here](http://www.kitsapairporter.com/client/assets/files/Bremerton%20Kitsap%20Brochure%2060661.pdf) (shuttles leave at 20 minutes past the hour, every hour), or at the ground transportation offices, from the BKA Desk in the South Arrival Hall at SeaTac, or from the shuttle drivers.

You’ll need to book shuttle reservations to return to the airport in advance, which you can do on the [Kitsap Airporter website](https://www.kitsapairporter.com/index.php/Reservations/KitsapSeaTac). Easy!
