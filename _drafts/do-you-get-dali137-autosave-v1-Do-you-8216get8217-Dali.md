Cat has officially taken her longest vacation in over half a decade. Yes, it's only been a week. Her work ethic is the kind of thing Europeans furrow brows and shake heads at. You can tell she was nearing burnout by this picture I snapped of her during a work videoconference just before our break: \[caption id="attachment\_140" align="aligncenter" width="640"\]![Underwear work](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/IMG_2019-1024x768.jpg "Cat working") Business up top, party down below.\[/caption\] In an effort to help recharge her batteries, I've been a most gentlemanly traveling companion. I've been doing all the ordering, asking, talking, paying. I mean, I speak French. She doesn't. She's been making up for that by repeating that she speaks some Spanish from the SEVEN YEARS she studied it. \[caption id="attachment\_139" align="aligncenter" width="640"\][![I've even been carrying all the heavy shit.](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/carry-1024x682.jpg)](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/carry.jpg) I've even been carrying all the heavy shit. With ice cream as fuel, of course.\[/caption\] So we arrived in España after a beautiful few days in Collioure, maybe my favorite town in the world. We had a couple hour layover until the next train left, I was expecting a break from doing everything all the time. That's when I realized that in her seven years of Spanish, she learned how to conjugate dozens of irregular verbs, but not how to ask for the bill at a bar. That is how we ended up drinking five beers instead of the two we wanted, and paying for seven. One for each year of the Spanish lessons that should have gotten us out of this situation in the first place. It reminds me of the month I spent traveling around Mexico with my dear friend, Matt Acosta. Matt is mustachioed and half-Mexican, but never learned a lick of Spanish. Every single person we met would go off on tirades of Spanish toward my unsuspecting comrade, after which he would awkwardly point to me and somehow nonverbally communicate that my Franco-American ass was their best chance at getting a few words across. I digress. We made it to the Teatro-Museo Dalì in Figueras, where we saw some wonderful art. \[caption id="attachment\_148" align="aligncenter" width="640"\][![Fairly tame, but a bit weird.](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/weird-1024x682.jpg)](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/weird.jpg) Fairly tame, but a bit weird.\[/caption\] \[caption id="attachment\_143" align="aligncenter" width="640"\][![Getting weirder...](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/IMG_2125-1024x768.jpg)](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/IMG_2125.jpg) Getting weirder...\[/caption\] As we walked through the museum, as the weirdness reached a mind boggling crescendo, Cat stopped and asked me seriously, "Do you get it?" "I don't think I ever 'get' art," I replied, "but I still like it..." This turned out to be the best joke I'd ever told, as Cat actually laughed out loud. I normally get nary a stronger exhale through the nose. Despite having not meant it as a joke, it was very gratifying. \[caption id="attachment\_144" align="aligncenter" width="640"\][![Weirdest. Seriously. What the fuck.](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/IMG_2132-768x1024.jpg)](http://www.duckandpotato.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/IMG_2132.jpg) Weirdest. Seriously. What the fuck.\[/caption\]
